About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, specializing in applied cryptography and theoretical computer science. My research focuses on privacy-preserving authentication, secure multiparty computation, and processing encrypted data. I aim to bridge rigorous cryptographic theory with practical systems, contributing to both foundational protocols and real-world applications.

Research Interests

Applied Cryptography (Decentralization, Privacy-Preserving Authentication, Processing Encrypted/Authenticated Data)
Theoretical Computer Science (Network Coding)
